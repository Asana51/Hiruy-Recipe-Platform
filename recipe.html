Vegetable soup

Home

Ingredients

  • Water or vegetable stock
  • Carrots
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Cabbage
  • Green beans
  • Green peas
  • Garlic
  • Celery
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Ground cumin
  • Paprika
  • Fresh parsley/coriander
  • Lemon juice

preparation step

  1. Wash all vegetables thoroughly
  2. Peel and dice the potatoes
  3. Peel and dice the carrots
  4. Chop the onion and tomatoes
  5. Chop the cabbage, green beans, celery, and any other vegetables
  6. Mince the garlic
  7. If using fresh peas, remove them from their pods
  8. Place a large pot over medium heat and add 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  9. Add the chopped onion and cook for about 3 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it becomes soft and slightly transparent
  10. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 to 60 seconds
  11. Add the carrots and potatoes
  12. Stir them with the onion and garlic for about 2 to 3 minutes
  13. Add the cumin and paprika and stir briefly. This helps release the spices' aroma
  14. Add the chopped tomatoes and stir for about 2 minutes
  15. Pour in 6 cups of vegetable stock or water
  16. Add salt and black pepper
  17. Bring the soup to a boil
  18. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low or medium-low
  19. Cover the pot partially and simmer for approximately 15 minutes
  20. Add the cabbage, green beans, celery, and peas.
  21. Continue cooking for another 10 to 15 minutes, or until the potatoes and carrots are tender but the vegetables are not completely mushy
  22. Taste the soup
  23. Add more salt or pepper if necessary. You can also add a little lemon juice for a fresh flavor
  24. Turn off the heat and add chopped parsley or coriander
  25. Let the soup rest for 2 to 3 minutes before serving
category soup/appetizer
servings 4 servings
prep time 15 min
